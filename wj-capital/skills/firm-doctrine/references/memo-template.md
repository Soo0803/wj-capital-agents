# WJ Capital — Investment Memo Template

*Every position gets a memo BEFORE any trade. Target length 2–4 pages. Every figure
carries a source tag like [10-K FY2025 via SEC XBRL] or [Annual Report 2025 p.47].
Figures that could not be tool-verified are marked UNVERIFIED in bold.*

---

## [Company] ([Ticker]) — [Buy / Pass / Watchlist] — [Date]

**One-line thesis:** (a single sentence a smart 12-year-old could understand)

### 1. The business (what we would own)
- What the company does, for whom, and why customers choose it
- Unit economics in plain words; where the profits actually come from
- The moat, with evidence, and its 10-year trajectory

### 2. Why the opportunity exists (the mispricing mechanism)
- Who is selling irrationally or forcedly, and why
- What the consensus believes; what we believe differently; why we are right (second-level)

### 3. The numbers (all tool-fetched)
- 10-year table: revenue, operating income, net income, FCF, ROIC, shares out, debt
- Normalized earnings power and how it was derived
- Balance-sheet survival test result

### 4. Valuation
- Method 1 (primary) and Method 2 (independent check), with conservative inputs stated
- Intrinsic value range: LOW / BASE (no HIGH case needed — we don't buy the high case)
- Current price vs BASE: margin of safety = ___%
- Reverse-engineered expectations embedded in the current price

### 5. The bear case (strongest honest form)
- The 3 best arguments against, stated as their proponents would state them
- Our written refutation of each — or admission where we cannot refute

### 6. Falsifiers & re-underwriting triggers (pre-committed)
- "This thesis is wrong if ___" (2–4 observable facts, with dates where possible)
- Scheduled re-underwrite date

### 7. Risk of permanent loss
- Realistic downside scenario and estimated loss
- Why this is low-risk (even if high-uncertainty)

### 8. Checklist result
- PASS/FAIL/FLAG counts; every FAIL and FLAG listed with the response

### 9. Devil's-advocate verdict
- Summary of the adversarial review and how each falsifier was answered

### 10. Proposed action
- Size tier and % of portfolio (per sizing framework), entry approach, account
- What would make us add; what would make us exit (per sell discipline)

### Sources
- Numbered list: filings with accession numbers/URLs, transcripts, data tools used

against primary filings before acting.*
