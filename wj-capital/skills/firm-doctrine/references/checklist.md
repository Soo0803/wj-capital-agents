# WJ Capital — Pre-Investment Checklist

*Pabrai-style: built from documented mistakes of great investors. The checklist-auditor
runs it as the FINAL GATE before any purchase. Every item is answered PASS / FAIL /
FLAG / N-A with a one-line justification and a source citation for factual items.
Any FAIL or 3+ FLAGS: the memo must explain why we proceed anyway, or we pass.*

## A. Circle of competence & thesis clarity (kill fast)
1. Can the thesis be stated in 4 sentences without spreadsheet references?
2. Do we understand how the business will look in 10 years (~90% confidence)? (Li Lu test)
3. Would we buy more if the price fell 50% on no new facts? (honest answer)
4. Is this inside the founder's written circle of competence? If new territory, has the
   study been done (reading 5–10 years of filings + competitors), not just excitement?
5. Is the thesis "low-risk, high-uncertainty" (good) or genuinely high-risk (bad)?
6. Why is this cheap? Can we name the forced/irrational seller or the market's error?
7. If we cannot name the reason for the mispricing — assume WE are the patsy. Pass?

## B. Business quality & moat
8. Does the company earn returns on capital above its cost across a full cycle?
9. What is the moat (brand, switching costs, network, cost advantage, regulation)?
   Cite evidence from filings/results, not adjectives.
10. Is the moat widening or narrowing? What did the last 5 annual reports show?
11. Pricing power: has the company raised prices without volume loss? Evidence?
12. Customer concentration: any customer >10% of revenue? (10-K disclosure)
13. Supplier/platform dependence (single fab, app store, one distributor)?
14. Is the product/service still needed in 10 years? What kills demand?
15. Disruption check: who is trying to destroy this business and how funded are they?
16. Cyclicality: where are we in this industry's cycle? Are margins peak or trough?
17. Unit economics: do incremental revenues carry higher or lower margins?
18. Does growth require heavy capital? (See's test: profit growth vs capital consumed)

## C. Financial statements & accounting red flags (forensic-accountant leads)
19. Revenue recognition: any change in policy, unusual acceleration, or channel stuffing signs?
20. Earnings vs operating cash flow: do they track? Persistent gap = flag.
21. Accruals ratio elevated vs peers/history?
22. Receivables or inventory growing faster than revenue for 2+ years?
23. Frequent "one-time" charges or heavy non-GAAP adjustments? (Adjusted-EBITDA abuse)
24. Capitalizing what peers expense (software, customer acquisition)?
25. Off-balance-sheet obligations: operating leases, purchase commitments, guarantees, SPEs?
26. Pension/other liabilities: assumptions reasonable?
27. Auditor: changed recently? Qualified opinion? Material weakness disclosed? (search filings)
28. Related-party transactions in the proxy/footnotes?
29. Goodwill vs market cap; history of impairments; serial-acquirer accounting?
30. Taxes: cash tax rate vs book rate — sustainable or a timing artifact?
31. Share count trajectory over 10 years: dilution machine or cannibal?
32. Any restatements in the last 10 years?
33. Foreign operations: currency translation, repatriation limits, VIE structures (China)?
34. For non-US names: audit quality, listing venue protections, disclosure standards — verified?

## D. Balance sheet & survival (leverage killed more value investors than anything else)
35. Net debt / normalized EBIT — can the company survive 2 years of trough earnings?
36. Debt maturity wall in the next 3 years? Refinancing risk if credit closes?
37. Covenants: what are they, how much headroom? (credit agreement exhibits)
38. Is any debt recourse to assets whose value falls exactly when the debt matters?
39. Off-cycle liquidity: undrawn revolver, unencumbered assets?
40. If revenue fell 30% for 18 months, does the company need external capital? (Pabrai's
    Delta Financial lesson: leverage + confidence = ruin)
41. Does OUR thesis require the credit market's cooperation? If yes, flag.

## E. Management & incentives
42. Track record over 10-20 years from filings (capital allocation record, not charisma).
43. Insider ownership: meaningful skin in the game? Recent insider buys/sells (Forms 3/4/5)?
44. Compensation structure: what behavior does it actually pay for? (proxy analysis)
45. Capital allocation history: buybacks below intrinsic value or at peaks? M&A record?
46. Promises vs delivery: compare 3-5 year old guidance/letters to what happened.
47. Any history of aggressive promotion, SPACs, serial equity raises, or litigation?
48. Related-party dealings, family employment, personal use of company assets?
49. Founder/controlled company: are minority shareholders protected or hostage?
50. Would we be comfortable if this CEO ran the company unsupervised for 5 years?

## F. Industry & competition
51. Industry structure: rational oligopoly or knife-fight? Capacity discipline?
52. Are competitors financially rational, or is someone burning venture/state money?
53. Regulatory risk: what single regulation change breaks the thesis?
54. Technology risk: what does AI/automation/substitution do to this business by 2035?
55. Input cost exposure: commodities, labor, tariffs — hedged or naked?
56. Union/labor relations history?
57. Geographic/geopolitical concentration (Taiwan, China revenue, sanctions exposure)?

## G. Valuation & expectations
58. All valuation inputs tool-fetched and cited? (No figure from memory — AI guardrail)
59. Normalized earnings power: what is a conservative mid-cycle number, and how derived?
60. Valuation by at least two independent methods (e.g., owner-earnings yield vs history/
    peers; asset/replacement value; sum-of-parts; conservative DCF as sanity check only).
61. Margin of safety ≥ 33% to conservative intrinsic value (≥50% for lower-quality)?
62. Reverse-engineer the price: what growth/margins does the CURRENT price imply?
    Are those expectations already pessimistic (good) or optimistic (bad)?
63. Downside case: if the thesis fails, what is realistic permanent loss? (Not "it can't")
64. "Heads I win, tails I don't lose much" — is the asymmetry actually present?
65. Have we checked our numbers against the company's own reported figures (not data
    aggregators) for the 3 most load-bearing inputs?
66. Base rates: what happens historically to companies in this situation (turnarounds
    mostly don't turn; fade rates for high-ROIC businesses)?

## H. Behavioral & process (Munger's misjudgment audit)
67. Are we cloning someone's position without doing our own work? (Cloning sources ideas,
    never conclusions.)
68. Commitment bias: have we already told people we like this stock?
69. Recency/anchoring: is the thesis anchored on the 52-week high or our first price seen?
70. Social proof: is the attraction that respected investors own it?
71. Authority bias: are we deferring to management's narrative or a guru's blessing?
72. Incentive check: who profits from us believing this story (promoters, bankers)?
73. Deprival effect: are we buying because "it got away" once before?
74. Have we written the BEAR case in its strongest form and can we refute it point by point?
75. Did the devil's-advocate review happen, and did we answer every falsifier in writing?
76. Boredom check: are we buying because cash feels uncomfortable? (Sitting is the job.)
77. Is there any urgency pressure ("closing window")? Urgency = flag. Wonderful businesses
    are wonderful next month too.

## I. Portfolio fit & structure
78. Position size per the sizing framework (see sizing-and-selling.md) — justified tier?
79. Correlation: does this add a bet we already have (same factor, customer, geography)?
80. Liquidity: days-to-exit at 20% of average volume — acceptable for our size?
81. Currency exposure understood and acceptable unhedged (or hedged deliberately)?
82. Tax and account placement considered?
83. If this becomes 40% of the portfolio through appreciation, are we comfortable holding?
84. What is the pre-committed re-underwrite trigger (date and/or events)?

## J. Final gate
85. Is the full memo written, with sources, downside case, and falsifiers?
86. Checklist complete with zero unexplained FAILs?
87. Has at least one night passed between the decision and the order? (No same-day buys.)
88. Journal entry drafted?
89. The founder can explain the thesis aloud, from memory, in under two minutes?
90. Would we be comfortable if this decision and its reasoning were published?
